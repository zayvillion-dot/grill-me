# Question bank

Seed questions by task type. Defaults in parentheses. Skip any that recon answers; pick the 3-5 most load-bearing per round.

## New build
1. What is the one observable outcome that means done? (a working happy path demoed end to end)
2. Who uses it first? (the requester only)
3. What is explicitly out of scope? (anything beyond the happy path)
4. Which existing code, data, or tools must it reuse? (whatever the repo already uses)
5. Hard constraints: stack, hosting, budget? (current stack, no new paid services)
6. What breaks or costs money if it is wrong? (nothing irreversible; ship behind a flag)
7. Who signs off before release? (the requester)

## New page
1. Who lands here and from where? (a first-time visitor from search)
2. What is the one action to drive? (a single call to action)
3. Replace an existing page or stand alone? (stand alone)
4. Which facts must appear, such as prices? (none shown; use "contact us")
5. Which photos and copy exist already? (none; use placeholders)
6. Deadline? (none; review before publishing)

## New offer
1. Who is it for, and what problem does it solve? (the existing core customer)
2. Price and how it is presented? (one price, shown plainly)
3. What is included and excluded? (a short fixed list)
4. How does it relate to current offers? (sits beside them, not replacing)
5. What is the guarantee or risk reversal? (none)
6. How will you know it works? (three sales in 30 days)

## New client
1. What do they want and by when? (their stated request, their date)
2. Who decides and who pays? (one contact, same person)
3. What is the scope boundary? (written scope, changes quoted separately)
4. Terms: deposit, payment schedule? (your standard terms)
5. What could make this client a loss? (unclear scope, slow approvals)
6. What do you need from them first? (access, assets, a signed agreement)

## Big change
1. What problem does the change fix, and how will you see it fixed? (a named failing case now passes)
2. What must not change? (public behavior, data)
3. Can it be done in steps and rolled back? (yes, small steps)
4. Who is affected and must be told? (the active users)
5. What is the backup plan? (revert to the last good version)
6. What is the deadline pressure? (none; correctness first)
